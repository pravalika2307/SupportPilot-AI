Where SupportPilot Breaks

Classification

Boundary confusion on multi-symptom queries (rebooting Watch classified as network).

Retrieval

Coverage gaps in rare storefront configs (Spanish App Store, top sim 0.407 < 0.450).

Grounding

Cross-domain mismatches (keyboard media keys matching iTunes billing) blocked by concordance gate.

Safety

Consequential financial-loss cases (e.g. $640 flight rebooking from alarm failure) missed by keyword triggers.

Methodology Disclosure
“70.50% is provenance-backed grounding coverage — not confirmed ticket resolution.”

SupportPilot located an auditable historical @AppleSupport interaction in the top-3 dense candidates scoring ≥ 0.450 similarity with concordant intent. There is no telemetric confirmation of permanent resolution. In operational reality, 70.50% of inquiries qualified for autonomous grounded drafting; 29.50% were safely withheld and routed to human specialists.
