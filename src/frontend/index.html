Critical Methodology Notice — Attribution Coverage vs. Ticket Resolution

The headline metric 70.50% Provenance-Backed Grounding Coverage measures attribution coverage: the agent identifies verified Apple support records meeting both semantic threshold (≥ 0.45 cosine similarity) and query-evidence intent concordance. 70.50% does NOT mean 70.5% of customer tickets are permanently resolved. A public social customer support dataset lacks device-level telemetry to confirm whether the user's issue was resolved without subsequent follow-up.

Failure Analysis & Fail-Closed Safety Philosophy

Enterprise customer support requires an asymmetrical loss function: an ungrounded hallucination or unauthorized policy promise is far more destructive than a precautionary human escalation.

1. Intent Concordance

Retrieved replies are disqualified if the historical case intent does not match the predicted customer intent, completely preventing cross-domain hallucinations.

2. Strict Similarity Floor

Any candidate scoring below 0.45 cosine similarity is rejected from autonomous dispatch, preventing tenuous or noisy keyword overlaps.

3. Context-Bound Stripping

Rejects any historical response referencing private DMs, specific timestamps (“in 10 minutes”), personal case numbers, or ungrounded repair promises.

4. Safe Precautionary Routing

When evidence is withheld or ambiguous, the agent abstains with code insufficient_grounding rather than speculating unsupported Apple policies.
