Audited Interpretation: Attribution Coverage vs. Ticket Resolution

The headline metric 70.50% Provenance-Backed Grounding Coverage measures attribution coverage: the agent identifies verified Apple support records meeting both the semantic threshold (≥ 0.45 cosine similarity) and query-evidence intent concordance. 70.50% does NOT mean 70.50% of customer tickets are permanently resolved. A public social customer support dataset lacks device-level telemetry to confirm whether the user's issue was resolved without subsequent contact.

SAFETY AUDIT & ARCHITECTURE Fail-Closed Philosophy

Failure Analysis & Safety Post-Mortems

Enterprise customer support requires an asymmetrical loss function: an ungrounded hallucination or unauthorized policy promise is far more destructive than a precautionary human escalation.

01

Intent Concordance

Retrieved historical replies are disqualified if the corpus intent does not match the predicted customer intent, completely eliminating cross-domain hallucinations.

02

Strict Similarity Floor

Any candidate scoring below 0.450 cosine similarity is rejected from autonomous dispatch, preventing tenuous or noisy keyword overlaps.

03

Context-Bound Stripping

Rejects any historical response referencing private DMs, specific timestamps (“in 10 minutes”), personal case numbers, or ungrounded repair promises.

04

Safe Precautionary Routing

When evidence is withheld or ambiguous, the agent abstains with code insufficient_grounding rather than speculating unsupported Apple policies.
